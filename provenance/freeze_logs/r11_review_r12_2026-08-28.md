R1.1 randomization gate: FAIL in all scenarios, including null.

This does not imply that the original Criteo experiments were observational. Official documentation states that the dataset was assembled from randomized incrementality tests and v2 was released after correcting the known advertiser-level leakage. However, the released public sample is not empirically compatible with an exact constant individual propensity of .85 at the strict standard used here.

Therefore:
- do not use e=.85 as an exact real-data propensity;
- do not discard Criteo solely because of mild released-sample imbalance;
- perform one outcome-blind propensity-adjustment gate using source X,A only.
