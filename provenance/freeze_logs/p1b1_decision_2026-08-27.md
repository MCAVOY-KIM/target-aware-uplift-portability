- 3,000/3,000 replications present.
- 12/12 scenarios × 250 reps.
- No duplicate task IDs, missing values, or infinities.
- Concatenated partial files exactly reproduce the aggregate replication file.

## Methodological decision
Do not:
- lower the 70% gate;
- reinterpret 67.73% as a pass;
- use a naive one-sided all-pairs confidence set.

Instead narrow the primary inferential family to the independently preselected source model's target portability, which is the actual deployment question.

The original all-model confidence set remains secondary and unchanged.
